Deep Research is for questions that need more than one search: it plans a set
of searches, reads what comes back, notes what it learned and where to dig
further, searches again from there, and finally writes a structured report with
its sources.

## Running one

In the composer, open **+** and switch on **Deep research**, then ask your
question. A Deep Research card follows the run: **Deep Researching...**, the
queries it generated, what it searched for and how many findings it took from
each round, then **Start writing final report...**. You can carry on in other
chats meanwhile; Actovia tells you when the report is ready.

The finished card says **Research completed in 6m · 42 sources** and holds the
report, with an **Activity** tab for the run and a **Sources** tab for every
page it used. **Download PDF** exports the report with numbered citations and a
**References** list.

If anything stops the run — Stop, a lost connection, a failed search — the card
ends as **Research failed** rather than waiting forever.

## Settings

**Settings → Deep Research**:

- **Breadth** — how many search queries to generate at each level (default 4).
- **Depth** — how many levels to follow leads down (default 2).

Larger values find more and take longer. Deep Research uses
[Web Search](/docs/tools/web), so it needs the Brave Search key.
