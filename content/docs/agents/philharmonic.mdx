Philharmonic is a second workspace beside Chat: instead of one assistant, you
message a **group** with a project manager (the **PM**) who breaks your request
into a plan, hands the steps to employees, follows their reports and tells you
how it went. Switch to it from the sidebar's title: click **Chat** at the top of
the sidebar and choose **Philharmonic** (and back the same way).

## Groups

**+ New group**, then describe what you need in the composer (**Message**
followed by the group's name). The PM
analyses it, recruits the employees it needs from your workforce, writes a
plan and delegates. The plan shows as a card with each step's status, and the
**Members** panel lists who is working on what (**busy** / **idle**). When the
PM needs something from you it asks in the transcript, and you **Reply to PM…**.

Each group has its own working folder for the files its members make. A group
can also be pointed at a folder of yours — **Open folder…**, or **New project…**
to start one — which is where [coding agents](/docs/agents/coding-agents) work.

## Composer commands (desktop)

Type `/` at the start of the composer to open the command menu. Keep typing
to filter it, use **↑ / ↓** to choose an entry, then **Enter** or **Tab** to
select it. **Escape** closes the menu. **Shift+Enter** adds a new line; Enter
used to confirm an IME candidate does not send the message.

| Command    | Action                                                                       |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `/compact` | Summarize older context and show the estimated token count before and after. |
| `/brief`   | Open the group's `AGENTS.md` for reading or editing.                         |
| `/status`  | Open the monitor with context usage and team activity.                       |
| `/archive` | Open the group's completed deliverables.                                     |
| `/stop`    | Interrupt the PM and the group's staff tasks.                                |

For `/compact`, wait until the current PM round finishes and enable context
management in **Settings → Memory**. Compaction keeps the latest six messages
alongside the summary, and the full conversation history remains available.
The group brief is loaded separately from this summary. If there is no older
context to absorb, the command reports that the context is already compact.
A failed or cancelled attempt leaves the previous summary available.

## Mention an employee (desktop)

Type `@` at a word boundary and start an employee's name to search active
employees. Choose one with **Enter**, **Tab** or a click. Each selection appears
as a recipient chip above the composer; click a chip to remove it. You can
select up to ten employees, then write your request and send it.

For example, select Avery and write **Review the report's sources before we
publish it**. The PM receives the selected employee IDs with your request and
coordinates the work through the group's plan, concurrency limits and review
process. Employee renames keep the recipient relationship intact. If sending
fails, your draft, attachments and recipients stay in the composer for retry.

## The group brief (desktop)

Each group has its own **AGENTS.md**: a durable brief containing the objective,
constraints, decisions, acceptance criteria and open handoffs. The first
request creates a starting brief. The PM can maintain it as requirements and
decisions evolve, and the PM, model-based employees and coding-agent employees
receive the current brief when starting a new round or task.

Open it with `/brief` or the **AGENTS.md** button above the composer. Choose
**Edit**, change the Markdown, then **Save** or **Cmd/Ctrl+Enter**. The preview
shows the saved version. If another edit arrives before yours is saved, your
draft stays available and the save reports a conflict. **Reload & discard edits**
loads the current version so you can review it before making a new edit.

The file lives in `~/.actovia/groups/<group-id>/AGENTS.md`. It belongs to the
group, separately from the `AGENTS.md` or `CLAUDE.md` in a folder you bind to it.
Use it for requirements and decisions that should survive context compaction;
keep routine progress updates in the conversation.

## Decision process

Under each PM message, **Decision process** opens the record of that round:
what triggered it (**You wrote** or **Scheduled**), whose reports it read, its
thinking, every step it handed out with the instructions it gave, any quota
refusal or wait, and the tokens the round used.

## Workforce

**Workforce** is where the team is defined. Every employee belongs to a
**Team**, whose system prompt applies to all its members. An employee has a
**Name**, a **Description**, its own **System prompt** on top of the team's,
and optionally:

- **Runs on** — **Actovia** (a model of yours) or a coding-agent CLI.
- **Model** — its own model, or the app's default.
- **Skills** and **MCP servers** it may use (none selected means all).
- **Memory (read-only)** — what it has learned over past work.

## Dashboard

**Dashboard** shows active and recent execution plans, recent plan activity,
and the distribution of steps by status. Open **Scheduled** from the Chat
sidebar to manage scheduled work. See [Schedules & quotas](/docs/agents/schedules)
for scheduling and coding-agent quotas. A PM round's token usage is in its
**Decision process**.

## On iPhone

The iPhone app has Philharmonic too, as the same workspace switch at the top of
its sidebar. It is for the part of a long run that needs you while you are away
from the desk: follow a group live as the plan ticks and reports arrive, reply
to the PM or answer its question, **Allow** or **Deny** a coding agent's step,
stop a round, and start a new task, photos included. **Members**, the
**Dashboard** and the **Workforce** are there to read; setting up employees,
teams, schedules and a group's folder stays on the computer.
