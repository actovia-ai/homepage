The iPhone app comes with widgets under **Actovia** in the widget gallery:

- **Ask Actovia** for the Home Screen (small and medium) — a greeting over the
  sky of the hour, **Ask Actovia…** to open a new chat with the keyboard up, and
  suggestions such as **Plan my day** that fill the composer without sending.
  The medium size also lists your **Recent** chats. With Health notes on, the
  top line shows today's sleep and steps.
- **Ask Actovia** for the Lock Screen (round and rectangular) — today in a
  line, and a way to ask.
- **Ask Actovia** control — for Control Center and the Action button: a new
  chat in one press.

Chat titles and health numbers are hidden on the Lock Screen and in StandBy
until the phone is unlocked, and the widgets empty when you unpair. They show
no message content, only titles. Tinted and clear Home Screens get Ody's
outline instead of the sky.
