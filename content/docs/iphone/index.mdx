Actovia for iPhone is a remote for the desktop app. It keeps no chats of its
own: every message goes to your computer, runs there with your keys and tools,
and streams back. A chat started on the phone is on the computer too, and the
other way round.

## Pairing

The first time you open the app it offers **Scan pairing code** and **Paste
pairing link**. On the computer, open **Settings → Integrations → Devices →
Pair a device** and scan the QR code. The phone needs a passcode, and the
connection is unlocked with Face ID. At home it connects over your network;
away, over Tailscale. How pairing works and how to unpair are on
[Remote access](/docs/remote-access).

A phone can be paired with more than one computer — a Mac mini at home and a
laptop, say. **Settings → Computer** lists them: tap one to switch, swipe to
unpair, **Pair another computer** to add one.

## Chat

A slide-out drawer (swipe from the left edge) holds **Recents** and search;
the chat beside it works as on the desktop — streaming answers with the
thinking timeline, citations and sources, tool cards (weather, maps, images,
artifacts, files, Computer Use, Deep Research), questionnaires and
confirmations, regenerate and compare, and the memory lines under an answer.
Approval cards can be answered from the phone.

The **+** in the composer adds pictures from the **Photo Library** or **Take
Photo** (up to ten), sets **Reasoning**, switches on **Deep Research**, and lists
the **MCP Tools** your computer offers. The microphone records a voice message and
transcribes it into the field.

## Settings

The phone edits the computer's settings directly, grouped as on the desktop:
**Color tone**; **Connection** and **Computer**; **AI Providers**, **Built-in
Tools**, skills and **MCP Servers**; **Personality** and **Memory**;
**Profile** and **Backups**. Saved keys show as masks here too.

## Locks

- The phone's own lock: after a while in the background the app asks for Face
  ID before it reconnects — **Actovia is locked** → **Unlock**.
- The computer's [app lock](/docs/app/app-lock): while the computer is
  locked, a paired phone can unlock it with its own Face ID.

## Languages

The app follows your iPhone's language: English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan
and Hong Kong), Japanese, Korean, French, German, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese
and Italian.

The iPhone app also has [Health](/docs/iphone/health) and
[widgets](/docs/iphone/widgets), which the desktop does not.
