**Health** is a workspace of the iPhone app (open the drawer, then **Health**).
It reads Apple Health — sleep, activity, heart and recovery, body and mood — and
shows your day with Ody, whose mood follows it: **Well rested**, **On the move**,
**A little under-slept**, **Taking it easy today**…

## Your data and the daily note

**Connect Apple Health** asks iOS for read access. Your health data stays on
the iPhone. If you agree to **May Ody write you notes?**, a short summary of
the day's numbers — never the raw data — goes to the AI provider set up on your
computer, which writes a note about your day. Notes are kept on the phone only,
encrypted while it is locked and never backed up. Turn the notes off, or
**Clear archive**, from **Health options**. Pull down to have the note written
again.

## Calendar and reports

The **This week** card opens a calendar of past days by **Week**, **Month**,
**Quarter** or **Year**, with the trend of each. Tap a day for its numbers and
the note it got. After each finished week, month, quarter and year, a report
is written on the next visit — a headline, insights, a comparison with the
period before, an idea to try — shown on the calendar and on its own page.

## Asking about it

**Ask about your day…** sends a question to a new chat with your numbers
attached (**Why do I feel tired today?**, **Should I train hard today?**), and the
answer works from them. The chat shows what was sent as a small health card.
Detail pages, past days and reports have their own suggested questions. When a
note says something worth keeping, **Remember** adds it to your
[memory](/docs/memory).
