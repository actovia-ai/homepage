## Quick Chat

Click Actovia's icon in the menu bar (Ody's head) and a small Quick Chat bar
appears on the screen your pointer is on: **How can I help you today?** Type
and press Enter — the main window comes forward and the question starts a new
chat there. Click the icon again to put the bar away. Right-click the icon for
**Show App** / **Hide App** and **Quit Actovia**.

Turn the menu bar icon off in **Settings → General → Menu bar**.

## Search chats

**Search chats** in the sidebar (⌘⇧F) finds a chat by its title or by anything
said in it. Each result shows the chat and a few matching snippets; pick one to
open it. With an empty search the dialog lists your recent chats.

## Find in page

⌘F opens a find bar under the header: type, and matches are highlighted with a
count such as **1/3**. Enter and Shift+Enter move between them; Esc closes the
bar. It closes by itself when you switch chats.

## Export a chat

In the chat header, **Chat options → Export as Markdown…** or **Export as
PDF…** saves the conversation: your messages and Actovia's answers, with the
tools used and the pictures attached noted in place.
