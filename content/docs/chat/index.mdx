A chat is a conversation with the model you picked, carried out on your
computer with your own API key. The model can answer from what it knows or
reach for [tools](/docs/tools) — search the web, read a file, run a command —
and every step it takes is shown as it happens.

## Sending a message

Type into **Ask anything** on the home page and press **Enter**. **Shift+Enter**
starts a new line, and while an input method is composing (Chinese, Japanese,
Korean) Enter picks the candidate instead of sending. The reply streams in
place; a title for the chat appears in the sidebar a few seconds later.

- **Stop** ends a reply at once. What was written so far is kept, and you can
  send the next message straight away.
- While a reply is still being written, a second Enter shows **Please wait**
  instead of sending twice.
- You can leave a chat while it is answering. It carries on in the background,
  and a **Response ready** notice with **View** tells you when it is done.

## The thinking timeline

Above each answer, a timeline shows what the model did to get there: its
thinking, every search, file read and command, each as a row you can open.
While the run is going it says **Thinking…** or **Working…**; afterwards it
folds into a single line such as **Thought for 12 seconds**. A tool that failed
is a line of the timeline, not an error box.

## Answers

Answers are Markdown: headings, lists, tables, code blocks with a copy button
and syntax highlighting, quotes, links and maths (`$$…$$`). Prose stays prose —
`$200 - $300` is money, not a formula, and `19~32°C` is a range, not
strikethrough.

When a reply stops because the provider failed (a used-up quota, a bad key),
what was written is kept and the reason shows at its foot: **This reply stopped
with an error: …**. See [Answers & sources](/docs/chat/answers) for copying,
citations, regenerating and reading aloud.

## Long chats

A chat opens on its newest messages. Scroll towards the top and earlier ones
load in without your reading position jumping. On a long chat an outline on the
right lists your questions — including ones not loaded yet — and a click jumps
there. **Scroll to bottom** brings you back to the newest message.

## The sidebar and the header

- The sidebar's title says where you are — **Chat** or **Philharmonic** — and
  a click switches between them.
- **New chat** (⌘N), **Search chats** (⌘⇧F) and
  [**Artifacts**](/docs/tools/artifacts#every-artifact-in-one-place) sit at the
  top of the sidebar; ⌘B shows or hides it.
- The menu at its foot has **Settings**, **Get the apps** (the iPhone app,
  Health and the command-line tool), **Help & docs**, **What's new** and, once
  a PIN is set, **Lock Actovia**.
- Each chat's menu has **Rename**, **Favorite** (favourites get a group of their
  own), **Copy conversation ID** (see
  [recall across chats](/docs/memory/long-conversations#across-chats)) and
  **Delete**.
- The header shows **Back** / **Forward** arrows over the pages you visited
  (⌘[ and ⌘]), the chat's title, the model chip (see
  [Providers & models](/docs/chat/models)), the context ring (below),
  **Chat options** (rename, favourite, export as Markdown or PDF, delete) and
  the **Files, tools and sources** panel.
- ⌘W closes the chat on screen and takes you home. Nothing is deleted.

## How full the context is

Once a chat has answered, a small ring in the header shows how much of the
model's context the chat fills — hover it for the numbers. It turns amber when
the conversation reaches the point where older messages get
[compacted](/docs/memory/long-conversations).

Click it to open the panel on **Context**: one bar split into what the last
request carried — the system prompt, skills, MCP, tools, memory, summaries and
messages — with the free space beside it, the time it was measured and where
compaction starts. The panel's other tabs list the chat's **Files**, **Tools**
and **Sources**; a row takes you to the answer it came from.
