## Citations

When an answer draws on a web search or a page the model read, the claim is
followed by a small citation chip: the site's icon and name in small print.
Hover it for a card with the page's title, site and extract. A chip that stands
for several sources reads like **Reuters +2**; its card pages through them with
← and →.

Sources are numbered through the whole chat, not per answer: a source found in
the first answer keeps its number when a later answer cites it again.

**Sources** under an answer opens the chat's panel on that answer's sources,
split into those it cited and the others it found. A search that brings back
pictures or videos shows them as a gallery under the answer; pictures open in a
viewer.

## The action bar

Each answer ends with a row of actions:

- **Copy** — the answer as text. Citations become `[1]`, `[2]` … with a
  **References** list at the end, so the copy reads well anywhere you paste it.
- **Read aloud** — speaks the answer with OpenAI's text-to-speech (voice, speed
  and format under **Settings → Voice**).
- **Regenerate** — asks again (below).
- **Sources** — the answer's sources, as above.

## Regenerate and compare

**Regenerate** asks the same question again and shows the new answer **beside**
the previous one, so you can compare them. On a narrow window the two become
tabs, **Answer 1** and **Answer 2**. Pick the one to keep with **Use this one**;
the other is put away and the conversation carries on from your choice. If you
just send your next message instead, the newer answer that finished cleanly is
kept for you.

## Questions and confirmations

Sometimes the model needs something from you before it goes on. Instead of a
wall of text it can ask:

- **A questionnaire** — one question at a time (**Question 1 of 2**), with
  options, **Other…** for your own answer, **Previous** / **Skip** / **Next**,
  and **Anything else?** before **Submit**. Your answers appear as a card, and
  the questionnaire turns into a summary marked **Answered**.
- **A confirmation** — what it is about to do, with **Approve** and **Reject**
  (and an optional note). Nothing happens until you choose.

Answered blocks stay answered when you reopen the chat — on the desktop or on
the iPhone.

## Rich answers

A few kinds of content are drawn rather than written out:

- **Steps** — a how-to as a numbered card, with pictures where the search found
  them.
- **Stock charts** — a symbol's chart from TradingView, which opens the symbol's
  page in your browser. (TradingView sees the symbol and your IP address, as any
  embedded chart would.)
- **People, places and things** — a dotted name opens Wikipedia's summary and
  picture, in your language when Wikipedia has it; **Read more on Wikipedia**
  opens the article.

## Pictures from other sites

A picture an answer links to from a site that was not part of its own search
results shows as a placeholder with **Load image** until you click it, so a page
cannot make Actovia fetch something by itself.
