**Settings → Data Controls** looks after the data in
[`~/.actovia`](/docs/data).

## Backups

- **Automatic Backups** — a backup every day at 3:00 AM, kept in
  `~/.actovia/backups/`.
- **Back Up Now** — one straight away. **Last Backup** and **Recent Backups**
  show what you have.

The CLI does the same: [`actovia backup`](/docs/cli/reference#actovia-backup).
The iPhone app shows the backups too, under **Settings → Backups**.

## Export & import

- **Export Data** — a ZIP of your conversations, settings and other data, to
  keep or move. API keys and other secrets are left out, and generated images
  are not included.
- **Import Data** — restores from an export, **replacing** everything there is
  now. A backup is made first.

## Danger zone

**Delete All Data** erases conversations, memories, research and documents;
your settings and API keys stay. A backup is made first, and you type
**DELETE** to confirm.
