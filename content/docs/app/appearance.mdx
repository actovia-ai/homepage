Everything here is in **Settings → General**.

## Theme

**Theme** is **Light**, **Dark** or **System** (follows your computer's
appearance as it changes).

## Color tone

**Color tone** gives your conversations an accent: **Neutral** (black and
white), **Emerald**, **Blue**, **Violet**, **Rose**, **Orange** or **Yellow**. It
colours the send button, links, your message bubbles, citation chips and focus
rings — in chats and in Quick Chat — while the rest of the app stays neutral.
The iPhone app has the same tones in its own settings.

## Language

**Language** sets the interface language — **Auto (detect from system)** or one
of: English, 繁體中文（台灣）, 繁體中文（香港）, 日本語, 한국어, Français, Deutsch,
Español, Português (Brasil) and Italiano. The model answers in whatever language
you write in, whatever the interface is set to.

## Start-up and the menu bar

- **Run on startup** — start Actovia when you log in.
- **Menu bar** — show Actovia's icon in the menu bar, for
  [Quick Chat](/docs/chat/finding#quick-chat).
