**Settings → Developer → Logger** reads Actovia's log, kept as one file a day
in `~/.actovia/logs/`.

- Pick a **Date** and a **Scope** (the part of the app, such as `chat`).
- **Live** follows today's log as it is written.
- The search box (press `/`) takes words, `-word` to exclude,
  `scope:chat` and `trace:a1b2`.
- The chart above the list shows lines over time by level — **Error**,
  **Warn**, **Info**, **Debug**; click a level in its legend to filter.

Open a line for its details: the message, its fields, and for an error its
**Stack trace** pointing at the source. ↑/↓ (or k/j) move to the next line.
**Lines around** shows what happened just before, **Same trace** everything
that belonged to the same request, and **Copy for AI** copies the line with its
context ready to paste into a chat. **More actions** has **Open Directory**,
**Export** and **Clear All**.

The CLI reads the same log: [`actovia logs`](/docs/cli/reference#actovia-logs).
