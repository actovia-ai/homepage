Discover turns your [memory](/docs/memory) into a personal news feed on the
home page: a row of articles for each of the topics you care about, refreshed
about once a day. **Refresh** fetches it again on demand.

It is **off by default**. When you turn it on, your memory topics go to your AI
provider (to become search queries) and the queries go to Brave — the same
provider as [Web Search](/docs/tools/web), so it needs the Brave Search key.
Nothing leaves your computer until you turn it on.

In **Settings → Discover**:

- **Enable Discover**.
- **Topics** — how many topics to show (default 4).
- **Articles per topic** — how many articles in each row (default 3).
