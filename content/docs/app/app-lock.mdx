The app lock keeps Actovia closed to anyone at your computer. While it is
locked, the window is covered and the app answers no requests — not from Quick
Chat, the CLI or a phone — except an unlock. Background work (a running
research, a scheduled task) carries on.

## Setting a PIN

**Settings → General → Lock & Privacy**: **Enter a 6-digit PIN**, then
**Confirm your PIN**. Then choose:

- **Unlock with Touch ID** (Macs with Touch ID).
- **Auto-lock when idle** — **Off**, 1, 5, 15 or 30 minutes.
- **Lock on app launch**.
- **Lock on system sleep**.

Lock at any time with **File → Lock Now** (⌘L). Too many wrong PINs in a row
make you wait before trying again. **Remove lock** turns it off and asks for the
current PIN.

## Unlocking from elsewhere

A paired iPhone can unlock the computer with its own Face ID, and the
[CLI](/docs/cli/reference#actovia-lock) with the PIN. See
[Security](/docs/security#app-lock).
