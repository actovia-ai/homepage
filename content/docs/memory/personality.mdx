## Personality

**Settings → Personality** shapes every reply:

- **Base style and tone** — **Default**, **Professional**, **Friendly**,
  **Candid**, **Quirky**, **Efficient** or **Cynical**.
- Fine-tuning — **Warm**, **Enthusiastic**, **Headers & Lists** and **Emoji**,
  each **More**, **Default** or **Less**.
- **Custom instructions** — anything else about behaviour, style and tone.
- **About you** — a **Nickname** to be called by, your **Occupation**, and
  **More about you** (interests, values, preferences to keep in mind).

## Profile

**Settings → Profile** is your usage at a glance: your avatar (click to upload
one), **Lifetime tokens**, **Peak day**, **Current streak** and **Longest
streak**, a **Token activity** heatmap (daily or cumulative), **Activity
insights** — total chats, model requests, installed and active skills — and
your **Top models**.
