Actovia is a personal AI assistant that runs on your own computer. These pages
are the technical side: where things live, how the pieces connect, and what
every command does.

## The pieces

- **The desktop app** (macOS, Windows, Linux) is the hub. It holds your chats,
  memories, settings and API keys, talks to the model providers with your own
  keys, and serves everything else.
- **The iPhone app** is a remote for the desktop app. Once paired, it chats,
  edits settings and reads results through the computer — at home over the
  local network, or from anywhere over Tailscale.
- **The CLI** (`actovia`) works on the machine the desktop app runs on: it
  launches the app, pairs phones, manages skills and updates itself. It is
  what you use on a computer you reach only over SSH.
