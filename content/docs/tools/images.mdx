---
title: Image generation
description: Pictures from a description, kept on your computer.
icon: Image
---

Ask for a picture ("draw a cat in a hat") and the answer shows a frame that
shimmers while the image is made — **Generating image**, **Refining details** —
and the picture appears in the same frame when it is ready. Click to zoom; use
the download button or right-click to save or copy it.

Image generation uses OpenAI's image models with your OpenAI key. Under
**Settings → Built-in Tools → Image Generation** choose the **Model**, **Size**,
**Quality**, **Output Format**, **Background** and **Generated Counts** (1–10
images per request).

Every image is saved in `~/.actovia/media/` the moment it arrives, so it is
still there after a restart. Deleting the chat deletes its images. Exports do
not include them.
